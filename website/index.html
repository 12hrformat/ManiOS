About

Mani Linux is designed to be a single system you can trust for serious security work — and comfortable enough to run as a daily driver.

Cybersecurity & Research

A curated, maintained toolset for offensive and defensive work — without the bloat of "everything and the kitchen sink" distros.

CTF Ready

Fast to spin up, pre-configured for common CTF workflows, without fighting the OS to get to the challenge.

Transparent

What the system does, collects, and connects to should never be a mystery. Privacy by default.

Usable Daily

Security tooling shouldn't come at the cost of a distro you can actually live in. Curated over comprehensive.

Professional

Built to look and feel like a legitimate distribution — not a "hacker aesthetic" costume.

Minimal

Tools are chosen deliberately, not dumped wholesale. Every package earns its place.
